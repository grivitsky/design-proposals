01 Logo & identity

Three directions. Then one system.

The name stays. What changes is the standard around it: a visual identity that can hold the precision of computation and the complexity of biology at the same time. Three directions are developed far enough to judge — one of them experimental — and the chosen direction is refined into a logo system the partners can use without a designer in the room.

Included

  • Three distinct identity directions
  • One experimental direction, deliberately bolder
  • A decision the partners can defend
  • A refined logo system: wordmark, symbol, lockups
  • Clear space, and incorrect use
  • A short rationale in the fund’s voice

02 Website

A site the thesis can live on.

proximal.vc should be as precise as the firms it backs. The site states the thesis, introduces the team, and shows the portfolio, and it gives the fund a writing hub with an article template so publishing does not become a second project. It is built in Framer, and structured so the team can edit it themselves.

Included

  • Thesis, team, and portfolio pages
  • A writing hub with an article template
  • A Framer project the team can edit
  • Responsive layout in the identity’s type and color
  • Structure a partner can update in an afternoon
  • Titles, descriptions, and share metadata

03 Brand guidelines

A book people will actually open.

An identity slips the moment a second person has to guess. The brand book records the logo system, color, typography, imagery, and graphic elements, plus the usage decisions that come up in a deck, on the site, and in a one-page leave-behind.

Included

  • Logo system and incorrect use
  • Color and typography, with working specs
  • An imagery system for compute and biology
  • Graphic elements, and where they stop
  • Usage for web, presentation, and print
  • A book organized to scan, not to archive

04 Presentation template

Three narratives. One visual standard.

Fundraising, a first meeting, and the thesis itself should not each invent a new visual language. One editable template covers all three, with layouts for a written argument, a chart, and a diagram, so a new version still looks like Proximal.

Included

  • A fundraising deck
  • A company overview
  • A thesis deck
  • Layouts for text, charts, and diagrams
  • An editable file the team duplicates
  • A structure that keeps the next version in the identity

Summary & investment

What Proximal leaves with.

Four deliverables, one standard for how the fund shows up. An identity with a point of view, including a direction bold enough to reject. A website the team can edit, with a place to write. A brand book that keeps the second use as careful as the first. And a presentation template for the raise, the overview, and the thesis.

Founders decide quickly whether a firm is serious. This is how Proximal is remembered after the meeting.
